ACTION P04 — A DUEL WITH HER REFLECTION / 镜中较量. Target 12 seconds. A quiet expression comedy with deliberately timed mirror behavior.

One real full-body girl only. A small oval mirror, about 0.30H high, floats at her anatomical LEFT front, angled so the audience can see a correct partial head-and-shoulder reflection. The reflection stays inside the frame and has exactly her identity. Its intentional timing differences are specified below; random facial/anatomical drift is not acceptable. Establish normal reflection first, then break it, then restore it. Use separate body, mirror frame and controlled reflection layers. A correct reflection transform applies to the mirror content only, never mirror the actual character's hair ornament or tail.

0–2 s: mirror appears beside the waist/chest region. She turns approximately 20 degrees toward it, adjusts a hanging sleeve, and looks into it. The reflection correctly matches her pose and timing, establishing an ordinary mirror.
2–4 s: her real expression remains composed. The reflection's one lip corner rises and its eyes soften FIRST. Give the audience roughly 0.4 seconds to notice before she does. Her real eyes move toward the reflected mouth, then one eyebrow lifts and her head withdraws slightly. No new face in the mirror.
4–6 s: the real girl raises one index finger beside her own face, outside the mirror, as a dignified reminder to behave. The reflection copies the finger with a deliberately late beat and is still suppressing a smile. No fingers enter or pass through the mirror surface.
6–8 s: she watches sternly, pressing her closed lip line. The reflection looks warmly back. Her real eyelids soften first, then shoulders relax, then a small closed-mouth smile finally escapes. At this exact point the reflection becomes fully synchronized again. Hold the shared understanding for about 0.6 seconds.
8–10 s: she notices the viewer, glances toward them, lowers the finger and slightly raises her chin to regain composure. The reflection now synchronizes correctly; one small hair-ornament adjustment completes the beat. No fresh prank at the end.
10–12 s: the reflected picture clears into ordinary dark-violet glass and the mirror contracts locally to a small decorative sliver and exits. She faces front and settles naturally to C-compatible neutral. Final half-second stable.

Mandatory: correct initial synchronization, one leading smile, one delayed imitation, her genuine softened reaction, restored synchronization. Do not generate a free-standing twin, a full-body second girl, mismatched face, random smiling throughout, unstable mirror frame, green chroma reflected inside the glass, or a reflection that never returns to correct behavior. Preserve the intentionally subtle but readable timing at small display size.

ACTION P01 — PAPERS UNDER COMMAND / 万页归序. Target 14 seconds, one continuous locked-camera full-body performance. H means the standing character's visible height.

Memorable event: a poised dragon girl deliberately organizes seven windblown pages into a neat stack; her own connected curled tail quietly catches the one page she missed. She notices its help only after completing the main action and almost smiles before recovering her pride. This is NOT reading, holding a book, or a generic spell.

Props: exactly seven warm-ivory paper sheets with restrained dark-violet edges and abstract marks, each about 0.17H high. Their paths remain outside the face. Do not create hundreds of paper particles. Each sheet flexes and has a persistent identity. Use controlled prop animation if needed.

0–1.5 s: from neutral C, a small stack appears to her left and is disturbed by a brief gust. Her eyes find the first escaping page; one brow lifts. She takes a small supporting step rather than waiting motionlessly for effects.
1.5–3.5 s: seven pages make a short, readable turbulent movement. She turns her torso about 30 degrees, bends into her support leg and sweeps her RIGHT hand upward and outward from below waist level toward the side of her shoulder. Her small wings open just enough to steady her. Sleeves lag behind her hand. Pages progressively organize into an arc beside and above the shoulders, leaving her face clear.
3.5–5.2 s: her RIGHT palm stops outward. Pages decelerate into individual held positions instead of teleporting. Her head and shoulders stop before her hair tips. Hold the clear commanding silhouette for about half a second.
5.2–8.5 s: her LEFT palm receives a stack near the waist. Two fingers of her RIGHT hand gather the arc inward. SIX sheets visibly straighten, align and land one after another on the left palm. Show at least three distinct contacts and increasing stack thickness. Her gaze follows arc to stack.
8.5–10 s: the seventh sheet slips down outside her body. The outer curve of her ONE original tail coil swings slightly forward and supports the sheet. Keep the root connected and the original thickness/taper; do not grow a long prehensile tip or clamp the sheet between invented tail fingers. She is still watching the stack, then looks down and notices the tail.
10–11.8 s: her RIGHT hand takes the seventh sheet from the tail and places it on the stack. Her eyes briefly soften in surprise; a restrained smile almost appears, then she lifts her chin as if the result was entirely planned. Affection, not anger at her tail.
11.8–14 s: she gently squares the stack once between both hands. It locally contracts to a thin bookmark and exits between the hands, without fading the character. Her supporting foot returns, arms lower, wings and tail settle to C-compatible neutral. End with half a second stable.

Mandatory: turbulent pages -> deceleration into arc -> ordered physical stacking; visible tail support; delayed recognition and restrained warmth. Reject a clip where the pages merely vanish, become sparks, pass through a hand/face, or where she only waves. Preserve paper bending, finger-to-paper contact, wrist/shoulder weight, and staggered cloth follow-through. A good composition must remain intelligible at about 300 px visible character height.

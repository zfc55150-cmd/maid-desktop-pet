ACTION P06 — THE WEIGHTLESS TEA INCIDENT / 失重茶会. Target 12 seconds. A physical-comedy variation that must do more than the existing tea-sipping clip.

One small white porcelain cup with dark-violet trim, about 0.12H high. LEFT hand holds the cup throughout with a consistent handle orientation; RIGHT hand controls liquid. No tray, teapot or spoon. Exactly FIVE identifiable warm-amber tea droplets, with readable liquid surfaces, restrained transmission, subtle deformation and no fog. Largest droplet about 0.055H wide. They are tea, not jewels, glass beads or star particles. Use controlled separate liquid/cup-surface/occlusion layers if necessary; chroma-green reflections are unacceptable.

0–2 s: the cup locally appears at her LEFT hand near the waist and rises toward her closed lips. Before reaching her mouth, the tea surface bulges and FIVE droplets separate from it. The cup has not touched her lips yet.
2–3.5 s: her eyes find the hovering liquid; they open slightly. LEFT wrist quickly rights the cup, shoulders draw back a little, feet remain planted. The sleeve continues forward briefly after her arm stops; it does not pass through a droplet.
3.5–6.5 s: RIGHT index finger traces a short controlled arc above the cup. FOUR droplets follow the arc into the cup ONE AFTER ANOTHER. Each entry produces a small depression/ripple; the liquid level increases consistently. Her gaze tracks individual droplets, and surprise becomes concentration. Keep liquid identity and volume consistent.
6.5–8 s: the last droplet hangs about one finger-width outside the RIGHT sleeve. She raises one brow, pauses, withdraws the wrist slightly to clear the sleeve, and makes a precise guiding gesture toward the cup. The final droplet returns. No liquid passing through fabric or hand and no new droplet spontaneously appearing.
8–9.8 s: RIGHT hand lowers. LEFT hand again brings the cup to her CLOSED lips, touches the rim with a slight tilt, then separates, suggesting a small composed sip without open mouth or speech. She glances at the audience as if this was perfectly ordinary.
9.8–12 s: cup lowers to the waist and locally exits; LEFT hand returns to neutral. Shoulders, sleeves and hair settle in sequence. Tail does not add unrelated acrobatics. End C-compatible with a stable final half-second.

Mandatory: an unexpected release of liquid, a quick deliberate cup rescue, four sequential returns, the last droplet's near-contact with a sleeve, and her composed resumption of tea. Reject an ordinary static tea pose with floating lights, rigid gemstone droplets, shifting cup grip, infinite liquid, simultaneous disappearance of all droplets, or a missing expression change. Preserve the cause-and-effect timing and the handmade 2D finish.

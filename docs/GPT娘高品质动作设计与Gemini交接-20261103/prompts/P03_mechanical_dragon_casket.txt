ACTION P03 — A LITTLE DRAGON FROM THE CASKET / 星匣孵龙. Target 14 seconds. Precision craft followed by a tiny affectionate break in composure.

Prop: a rigid dark-violet mechanical casket, closed width about 0.18H. A quarter-turn ring lock and central latch have fixed geometry. Six hinged shell petals open into a warm-gold/dark-violet miniature mechanical dragon, total length about 0.28H. The dragon has one head, two wings, four legs and one tail, and is clearly separate from the girl's original white tail. It is a temporary performance prop, not a permanent new companion. The dragon needs readable solid forms, not a glowing blob. Use stable prop models/controlled animation if needed.

0–1.5 s: her LEFT palm rises. The closed casket locally appears and rests on the palm with visible contact. Her RIGHT hand approaches the ring lock; gaze becomes attentive. Keep the face unobstructed.
1.5–4 s: RIGHT thumb and index finger turn the ring approximately one quarter turn and press the center latch. LEFT hand stabilizes. She leans a little closer; sleeves respond to the wrist. Fingers must remain outside the solid casket except at the actual contact surface.
4–7 s: the shell unlocks. Six petals hinge outward in a left-right sequence. The small dragon's head lifts, then wings, feet and tail unfold in that order. Each part follows a persistent hinge and comes to a stop; no liquid morphing into a random animal. Her receiving palm dips slightly under the transferred weight; eyes follow the head.
7–9 s: the little dragon makes one short wing beat, lifts just above the palm, then lands at its edge. Her RIGHT index finger approaches. The dragon touches its nose gently to that fingertip, then looks up at her. One precise contact target, no random orbit around her head.
9–10.5 s: she had begun to lift her chin proudly. The unexpected nose-touch makes her eyes widen very slightly and her finger pause. Her restrained smile softens, with a small relaxation in her shoulders/wings. No exaggerated blush graphic, open mouth or laughter.
10.5–12.5 s: she gently strokes the side of its brow once, then gestures toward the casket center. The dragon draws in its feet, folds wings, lowers its head, and mechanically folds back in a deliberately staged sequence. This is newly performed closure with her gaze/hand continuing naturally, not reversed video.
12.5–14 s: shell closes. The small closed casket exits locally from her left palm. She glances toward the viewer, restores her composed expression, lowers her hands and settles to C-compatible neutral. Final half-second stable.

Mandatory: finger-operated unlock; readable hinged unfolding; nose-to-finger contact; pride giving way to a little warmth; controlled closure. Reject a static girl holding a glowing gem, a dragon teleporting into frame, changing mechanism topology, floating contacts, fused fingers, or a second character tail accidentally attached to the girl.
